## 4. Business Segments (6 Sectors)

1. **Residential & Commercial:** Fast-cycle table forms, aluminum drop-head slab panels, modular wall forms for towers and compounds.
2. **Industrial Facilities:** High-capacity shoring, silo forms, heavy equipment foundations, and process plants.
3. **Infrastructure (Bridges & Tunnels):** Heavy-duty shoring trusses, parapet carriages, travelling tunnel shutters, and pier head systems.
4. **Oil & Gas:** Blast-resistant shuttering, circular tank formwork, cryogenic storage bases, and certified industrial scaffolding.
5. **Landmark High-Rise:** Automatic hydraulic self-climbing core walls, cantilever safety screens (BPS), and architectonic concrete shutters.
6. **Power & Water:** Dam shuttering, water treatment basin forms, turbine foundations, and desalination plant structural solutions.

## 6. End-to-End Integrated Services

1. **Engineering Solutions:** 3D BIM modeling, structural finite element analysis (FEA), formwork deployment sequencing, and customized design optimization.
2. **Formwork Sale:** Direct procurement of newly fabricated components built to British Standards (BS 5975) and European Norms (EN 12810 / 12811).
3. **Formwork Rental:** Immediate availability across 20 regional yards, reducing contractor CAPEX and enabling flexible project scaling.
4. **Site Technical Support:** On-site certified temporary works engineers supervising erection, pre-pour inspection, and safety verification.
5. **After-Sales & Refurbishment:** Complete reconditioning, laser alignment, plywood replacement, shot-blasting, and protective recoating.
6. **Global Logistics:** Multi-modal freight coordination ensuring just-in-time delivery to remote construction zones.

## 7. Landmark Project Portfolio

* **Rod El-Farag Cable-Stayed Bridge (Cairo, Egypt):** Guinness World Record holder for the widest cable-stayed bridge (67.3 m wide). ACROW provided heavy shoring trusses and custom climbing pylon carriages.
* **Iconic Tower & CBD (New Administrative Capital):** Africa's tallest skyscraper (394 m). ACROW provided auto-climbing core wall systems, Alu-Deck slab panels, and heavy shoring.
* **Algiers Metro Extension (Algiers, Algeria):** Tunnel travelling formwork and subterranean station vault falsework.
* **Benban Mega Solar Park (Aswan, Egypt):** High-speed modular foundation shuttering for one of the world's largest solar installations.
* **Midor Oil Refinery (Alexandria, Egypt):** Circular tank formwork, blast walls, and specialized access scaffolding.
